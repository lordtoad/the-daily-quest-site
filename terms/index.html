What the app does

The Daily Quest helps you organize personal actions into quests, schedule them and record progress. A Guild groups related quests; it is not a public social group. Experience points, levels and other progress indicators describe activity recorded in the account edition. They have no payment or cash-redemption function.

The app is a personal planning and tracking tool. It is not a medical service and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent a medical condition. Seek qualified professional advice for medical decisions.

Your account and journal

The upcoming edition uses Google sign-in; Apple sign-in is deferred. Use an account you are authorized to access, and protect access to your device and Google account. The app does not ask for your Google password.

Account journals synchronize through Supabase. First sign-in and loading need internet. After loading, edits can save on that installation while offline; synchronization waits for a connection and the backend. A local save does not confirm that another device has received the change.

Review a conflict before choosing which journal to keep. Recovery versions are available for 30 days. Export JSON to keep an independent copy longer. CSV reports cannot restore a journal. Signing out, clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove unsynchronized edits, so export anything you want to keep first.

Older local-only journals have an export option and a separate choice before adoption into an account. Do not upload another person's private information without authority. Do not access another user's account or bypass service security controls.

Free features and testing

The account-edition test release has no in-app purchase, subscription or payment-details collection. Extended history and progression can be unlocked for free. The first unlock requires a connection and is recorded for your app account, separately from a journal's JSON backup. This test release's free access does not describe an unannounced future release's pricing or entitlement.

Features and data handling can change in later versions. Service interruptions or capacity limits can delay sign-in, synchronization and other online actions. Keep an export of important records; synchronization and recovery copies do not promise permanent backup or uninterrupted availability.

Privacy, reminders and deletion

The account-edition privacy draft explains account data, journal storage, optional metrics, retention and deletion. The local-only privacy policy continues to apply to available builds through 1.0.8.

Optional usability metrics start off and can be turned off in account settings. Reading Terms, signing in or unlocking free access does not enable metrics or confirm upload of an older journal. Those choices remain separate.

Device reminders are optional. Device settings and operating-system restrictions can delay delivery.

The account edition is designed to offer deletion in Manage → Account → Delete account and on an authenticated website. See account-deletion availability; the web request tool is not available in this preview. The deletion process removes the app account's journal and free-access records as described in the privacy policy. It does not delete your Google account or exported copies. Uninstalling or signing out does not request cloud account deletion.
