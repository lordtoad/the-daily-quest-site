Privacy policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

The Daily Quest (`com.lordtoad.thedailyquest`) is published by Raul Figueiredo Pazetto, publishing as LordToad. Contact: lordtoad.apps@gmail.com.

Your journal

Guilds, quests, schedules, targets, progress and completion timestamps stay in the app's local storage. The publisher does not operate a journal server or collect your journal. There are no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking or automatic journal synchronization.

Android automatic app backup is disabled. The iOS app is configured to exclude its internal storage from automatic backup; device backup verification is part of pre-release testing. Export JSON before changing devices. Browser, Android and iPhone installations keep separate journals.

Exports and files

Only when you choose, the app saves CSV reports or JSON backups, imports a selected JSON file, or opens your device's share sheet. It does not request broad file access. Your selected storage provider or destination app may store or transmit the export under its own policy. Shared temporary files are private to the app; files older than 24 hours are removed when another export is prepared. The operating system may clear temporary files sooner.

Retention and deletion

Your journal remains until you replace it through restore, clear app/site storage, or uninstall the mobile app. Files exported elsewhere remain under your control and must be deleted separately. No online account exists to delete. Keep an exported backup if you need to recover your journal after uninstalling or changing devices.

Support email

If you email lordtoad.apps@gmail.com, the publisher receives your email address, message and attachments to respond to your request. Email is handled by Gmail under Google's privacy policy. Do not send a journal backup unless you intend to share it. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and follow-up; you can ask for deletion by email, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Public website

The support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. We add no analytics, advertising, contact forms or tracking cookies. GitHub receives ordinary connection data, including IP addresses, to serve and secure the site under its privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Visiting this website does not upload the app's journal.

Permissions and updates

The app works offline from first launch. It does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone or notifications. Material privacy changes will be described here and in the app.
