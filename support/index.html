The support desk

Need a hand with your quest journal?

Email lordtoad.apps@gmail.com. Include your device model, operating-system version, app version and what happened. Do not send passwords or your journal unless you intend to share it.

Move your journal

In Manage, choose Back up journal and save the JSON file. On the new installation, choose Restore backup and select it. Restoration replaces the destination journal after confirmation. CSV is for reports, not restoration.

Before uninstalling

Export JSON first. Your journal is stored locally and cannot be recovered by the publisher. Exported files remain wherever you saved them.

Record a missed day

Use the previous-day arrow or date picker on the board, then record progress as usual. It counts toward the selected scheduled day.
