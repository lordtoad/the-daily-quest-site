Version 1.0.12: What's new

September 30, 2026 · Build 13

Available to Google Play internal testers. Google Play confirmed build 13 on September 30, 2026. Eligible testers can open the internal testing page. Closed Alpha remains on build 10; production is unchanged.

  • Guided creation: Follow optional explanations in real quest and Guild forms, with explicit saves and retained drafts.
  • Screen overviews: Explore Dashboard and Manage with Next, ending at Free access without activating it.
  • Contextual help: Replay Guild Master guidance, hold New quest to open help, and receive gentle onboarding cues.
  • Mobile presentation: Read anchored explanations with smoother transitions, accessible controls, and reduced-motion support.
  • New-journal defaults: Draw up to three Side quests by default when starting a new journal.
  • Story presentation: See refreshed Guild Master dialogue, reward layouts, and the illustrated sign-in entrance.

Important details

  • Saved progress: Existing journals, XP, and draw preferences are preserved; guides never create entries or log progress automatically.
  • Help history: Guidance preferences stay on each device per account and are excluded from backups and sync.
  • Languages: Guidance supports English and Brazilian Portuguese.
  • Compatibility: No new journal schema is introduced; upgrades from 1.0.9 include schema-v3 changes, so retain a backup and update every device before editing.
  • Testing: Physical-device acceptance remains pending; production and Closed Alpha are unchanged.

Version 1.0.11: What's new

September 30, 2026 · Build 12

Superseded internally by build 13. Google Play first confirmed build 12 on September 30, 2026. Eligible testers can open the internal testing page. Closed Alpha remains on build 10 while backed-up device acceptance is completed.

  • Brazilian Portuguese: Use translated screens, onboarding, Guild story, reminders, and app messages.

  • Language boundaries: CSV headers remain English; Google and Android screens and external pages follow their own settings.
  • Availability: Closed Alpha promotion requires backed-up Play upgrades and two-device acceptance; production access is not available.

Version 1.0.10: What's new

September 30, 2026 · Build 11

Superseded internally by build 13. Google Play first confirmed build 11 on September 30, 2026. Eligible testers can open the internal testing page. Closed Alpha remains on build 10 while backed-up device acceptance is completed.

  • Guild story: Meet the Guild Master, choose daily starter quests, and take your personal oath.
  • Saved rewards: See completion stamps, card exchanges, and ordered Quest, Guild, and Adventurer level-up chapters.
  • Personalization: Edit your oath, replay the story, and unlock Guild Wall, Hearth Hall, and Clocktower Night backgrounds.
  • Weekly targets: Count sessions on distinct days and earn a 10 XP bonus when you reach the weekly target.
  • Side quests: Choose a specific eligible Side quest to draw.
  • Cleaner screens: Expand quest descriptions when needed and open Dashboard after your first credited completion.
  • Simpler tracking: Record progress directly without cues or check-in controls.
  • Accessibility: Use reduced motion, separate sensory settings, keyboard oath controls, or the no-hold alternative.

Important details

  • Progress: Existing quests, completion history, and XP are preserved; story replay does not duplicate quests or reset progress.
  • Removed fields: Older cue and check-in fields are discarded during migration; save an old JSON backup first if you need those fields.
  • Compatibility: Update every device on the same account before editing; older app versions cannot read new schema-v3 journals or backups.
  • Weekly access: Weekly targets require Adventurer Level 2 and the account's free feature unlock.
  • Preferences: Sound, vibration, celebrations, and motion settings stay on each device; reduced motion suppresses decorative movement and vibration.

  • Accounts: Sign in with Google and keep your quest journal synchronized across devices.

Version 1.0.5: What's new

September 10, 2026 · Build 6

Available to Google Play internal testers. Enrolled testers can open the internal test page to update.

  • Quest categories: Choose Main or Side quests; board groups and Dashboard tabs show each category separately.
  • Priority: Reorder quests in Manage without changing past boards.
  • Re-rolls: Swap incomplete random quests once per slot per day.
  • Editing: Update today and future entries immediately while preserving past records.
  • Check-ins: Add Main quest routine or time cues and optional reflection prompts every three days or weekly.
  • Android reminders: Set daily board and combined Main quest reminders, times, and weekdays in Manage → Notifications.
  • Targets: Set minimum or maximum numeric targets, supported in the Dashboard, JSON backups, and CSV reports.
  • Backups: Keep your journal local and save quest details, priority, re-roll history, and check-ins in JSON backups.

Important details

  • Main quests: Existing quests start as Main; focus completion counts Main quests only.
  • Keyboard ordering: Press Space to pick up or drop, arrows to move, and Escape to cancel.
  • Re-roll eligibility: Replacements must occur later that week in the same Guild and category; completed quests and quests with a check-in cannot be re-rolled.
  • Re-roll progress: Both progress records restart; no eligible replacement means no allowance is used.
  • Tracking changes: Changing a quest's tracking type resets its current progress.
  • Reflection: In-app check-in answers never change progress or completion.
  • Notification setup: Both reminders start off; allow notifications and use Test notification to check delivery.
  • Reminder delivery: Android settings may delay device-scheduled reminders; quest time cues do not create notifications.
  • Main reminders: Cover generated days in the current week and refresh when you open the app.
  • Reminder preferences: Stay on this installation and are excluded from journal backups.
  • Maximum targets: Log a value to complete the quest, including an explicit zero when applicable.
  • Before updating: Save a JSON backup from Manage; older backups remain supported. See the support guide.
  • Update availability: Google Play may take time to show the update on your phone.
