Version 1.0.5: What's new

September 10, 2026 · Build 6

Available to Google Play internal testers. Enrolled testers can open the internal test page to update.

  • Quest categories: Choose Main or Side quests; board groups and Dashboard tabs show each category separately.
  • Priority: Reorder quests in Manage without changing past boards.
  • Re-rolls: Swap incomplete random quests once per slot per day.
  • Editing: Update today and future entries immediately while preserving past records.
  • Check-ins: Add Main quest routine or time cues and optional reflection prompts every three days or weekly.
  • Android reminders: Set daily board and combined Main quest reminders, times, and weekdays in Manage → Notifications.
  • Targets: Set minimum or maximum numeric targets, supported in the Dashboard, JSON backups, and CSV reports.
  • Backups: Keep your journal local and save quest details, priority, re-roll history, and check-ins in JSON backups.

Important details

  • Main quests: Existing quests start as Main; focus completion counts Main quests only.
  • Keyboard ordering: Press Space to pick up or drop, arrows to move, and Escape to cancel.
  • Re-roll eligibility: Replacements must occur later that week in the same Guild and category; completed quests and quests with a check-in cannot be re-rolled.
  • Re-roll progress: Both progress records restart; no eligible replacement means no allowance is used.
  • Tracking changes: Changing a quest's tracking type resets its current progress.
  • Reflection: In-app check-in answers never change progress or completion.
  • Notification setup: Both reminders start off; allow notifications and use Test notification to check delivery.
  • Reminder delivery: Android settings may delay device-scheduled reminders; quest time cues do not create notifications.
  • Main reminders: Cover generated days in the current week and refresh when you open the app.
  • Reminder preferences: Stay on this installation and are excluded from journal backups.
  • Maximum targets: Log a value to complete the quest, including an explicit zero when applicable.
  • Before updating: Save a JSON backup from Manage; older backups remain supported. See the support guide.
  • Update availability: Google Play may take time to show the update on your phone.
