September 10, 2026 · Version 1.0.5

More room for your everyday adventures

Choose your focus, make your board your own, and pause to reflect along the way.

Upcoming internal test release. Version 1.0.5 is being prepared for existing internal testers on Google Play.

Focus on what matters

Choose Main or Side when posting or editing a quest. Your board groups them separately, and its focus completion counts Main quests. Dashboard tabs let you review each category. Existing quests start as Main.

In Manage, drag quests into priority order. You can also use the keyboard: Space to pick up, arrows to move, Space to drop, and Escape to cancel. Your past boards keep their order.

A little flexibility for today

Re-roll an incomplete random quest to swap it with an eligible quest later in the same week, in the same Guild and category. Both progress records restart. Each slot can be re-rolled once per day; completed quests and quests with a check-in are protected. If no replacement fits, your allowance stays available.

Editing a quest now updates today and future entries immediately while preserving past records. Changing the tracking type resets its current progress.

Remember, then reflect

Main quests can have an optional routine cue or a chosen time. Enable follow-ups every three days or weekly to receive optional check-in invitations inside the app. Reflect on your cue, barriers and how you felt. Answers never change your quest progress or completion.

For Android reminders, open Manage → Notifications. Enable a daily board reminder, a combined Main quest reminder, or both, and choose their times and weekdays. Both start off. Allow notifications when prompted and use Test notification to check delivery.

Reminders are scheduled on your device, subject to Android settings and delays. Main reminders cover generated days in the current week and refresh when you open the app. Quest time cues do not automatically create notifications. Reminder preferences stay on this installation and are not included in journal backups.

Track your own targets

Numeric quests support minimum and maximum targets. Maximum targets require a logged value, including an explicit zero. Your dashboard, JSON backups and CSV reports respect these rules.

Keep your journal with you

Your journal stays local. JSON backups retain the new quest details, priority, re-roll history and check-ins; older backups remain supported. Before testing an update, save a JSON backup from Manage. Read the support guide for backup and restore instructions.
