Small steps. Worthy adventures.

Turn everyday actions into quests, group related quests into Guilds, and keep a record of your progress.

Available to closed Alpha testers

Version 1.0.8 (build 9) is the available closed Alpha release. It keeps your journal on your device, works offline and has no accounts, advertising or analytics.

Open the Alpha testing page

For eligible testers in the existing closed test. Check release notes and availability before installing or updating.

A journal for small, repeatable actions

  • Choose an action: Give your quest a clear name and decide which days it repeats.
  • Record what you did: Mark a quest complete or record progress for its scheduled day.
  • Keep your history: Save a full JSON backup or export a CSV report.

Upcoming: an account-based journal

Version 1.0.9 is being prepared and is not yet available. It will use Google sign-in to synchronize journals through Supabase. Older local-only journals will have an export option and a separate confirmation before upload.

Optional usability metrics will start off. Signing in, reading the Terms or unlocking free features will not turn them on.

Read the draft account-edition privacy policy, draft Terms and upcoming account guide. These previews do not change version 1.0.8. Apple sign-in and iPhone distribution are deferred.
